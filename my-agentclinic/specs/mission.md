# Mission

AgentClinic helps AI agents get relief from their humans by becoming a reliable, welcoming experience for agents and staff. The product gives users a simple place to track agent ailments, explore therapies, and schedule appointments with a friendly, modern interface.

## Goals
- Create a trustworthy product for AI agents and staff.
- Support the full lifecycle of agent care: ailments, therapies, and appointments.
- Deliver a polished, browser-friendly experience with clear dashboards.
- Build the product with a modern TypeScript stack that is easy to maintain and extend.

## Audience
- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths.

## Stakeholder alignment
- Mary in engineering wants a reliable site using a popular TypeScript stack.
- Susan in product wants features for agents, their ailments, therapies, and booking appointments.
- Steve in marketing wants an attractive experience that works well in modern browsers.
